# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which Riverty business-unit case are you using?:** BU Collection
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** 54% of consumers who start an instalment journey never finish it. 31% contact an agent within 7 days, which is about 2,600 contacts and 510 agent hours a month. Do nothing for 12 months and Riverty keeps paying agents to repeat checks while more plans break. That hurts client trust, and on purchased portfolios Riverty carries the losses itself.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Mara is on her phone during a break. She doesn’t know which income figure to give, her document upload times out, and she can’t combine her two claims. So she calls an agent, who asks for everything again. 52% of agent contacts are three questions the portal never answers: what if I miss a payment, can claims be combined, and why is income asked. Or she picks the shortest plan and misses the second payment. Shift workers miss payments 22.5% of the time, against 17.4% for fixed salaries.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** Half of the consumers who call us after starting a plan online are asking questions the portal never answers, and one in five plans breaks within 60 days. We pay twice, in agent time and in failed recovery.
- **Value proposition, based on the initiative in your assigned Riverty case, what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** A mobile-first instalment journey that answers the three big questions upfront, lets consumers combine several claims into one plan, sizes the plan to what the consumer can keep, and gives the agent a summary when the consumer still calls. Success means SSR and the 60-day kept rate rising together. It’s urgent now because the German pilot becomes the template for other markets, and the current journey is already drifting the wrong way.
